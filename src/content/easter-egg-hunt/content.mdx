<EggCard title="The White Rabbit" slug="the-white-rabbit">

There is a place where you can search the whole site. Summon it, and instead of words, enter the number of the apartment where Neo lives. Watch what answers.

<EggSolution eggId="the_white_rabbit">

- Press ⌘K (or Ctrl+K) to open the command palette.
- Type 101 — the number of Neo's apartment.
- Follow the white rabbit.

</EggSolution>

</EggCard>

<EggCard title="Déjà Vu" slug="deja-vu">

A déjà vu is a glitch in the matrix — it happens when they change something. Find a page that tells a story, look to its header, and click… again, again, again, and once more.

<EggSolution eggId="deja_vu">

- Open any article or content page.
- Click the page header 4 times.
- Watch reality glitch.

</EggSolution>

</EggCard>

<EggCard title="I Know Kung Fu" slug="i-know-kung-fu">

Some codes from an older console never truly die. On any page, without clicking anything, walk up, up, then crouch down, down, dodge left, right, left, right, then punch, then jump. Something new gets downloaded straight into your head. Carrying a phone instead of a keyboard? There is also a tiny hidden spot tucked into the bottom right corner of every page, and it wakes up after five quick taps.

<EggSolution eggId="i_know_kung_fu">

- On any page, press ArrowUp, ArrowUp, ArrowDown, ArrowDown, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, b, a.
- On a phone or tablet, tap the very bottom right corner of the screen five times quickly instead.
- Either trigger works anywhere on the site, no click needed for the keyboard version.
- Watch the construct load, then know kung fu.

</EggSolution>

</EggCard>

<EggCard title="There Is No Spoon" slug="there-is-no-spoon">

A boy once warned Neo never to try bending it. Instead, only try to realize a simple truth about it. Then find the one thing on this site that answers when you talk to it, and tell it that truth.

<EggSolution eggId="there_is_no_spoon">

- Open the chat, from the menu or the command palette.
- Type there is no spoon and send it.
- Case and spacing do not matter, and the phrase can end a longer sentence.

</EggSolution>

</EggCard>

<EggCard title="The One" slug="the-one">

Everything starts with a question. Find the shell that lives on this site, and ask it the one question every Unix user asks when they have forgotten who they are.

<EggSolution eggId="the_one">

- Open the command palette and choose the terminal, or open /terminal directly.
- Type whoami and press enter.
- The answer was already inside you.

</EggSolution>

</EggCard>

<EggCard title="Dodge This" slug="dodge-this">

You cannot stop the rain, but you can bend it. Find the panel that controls it, and push it as fast as it will go.

<EggSolution eggId="dodge_this">

- Open the command palette and choose to customize the matrix rain.
- Push the speed slider all the way to its maximum.
- Dodge this.

</EggSolution>

</EggCard>
