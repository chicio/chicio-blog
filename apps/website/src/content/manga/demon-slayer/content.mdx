## <ParagraphTitleWithIcon icon={<FaBookOpen className="text-shadow-lg" />}>Plot</ParagraphTitleWithIcon>

Tanjiro Kamado is a young charcoal seller who lives quietly in the mountains of Taisho era Japan. One day he comes home to a tragedy: his family has been attacked by a demon, and his sister Nezuko, the only one still alive, has become a demon herself. Instead of giving up on her, Tanjiro sets out to find a way to make her human again. His path leads him to the Demon Slayer Corps, the secret order that fights these creatures in the dark, and to a journey made of training, friendships and swordsmanship.
